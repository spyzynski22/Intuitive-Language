Discover your intuitive language

You've heard of love languages. Your intuition has one too. It speaks through seeing, hearing, feeling, knowing, or smelling, and one of those is usually loudest. Find yours, and you stop second-guessing and start recognizing it when it shows up.

  1. Read through the five Clairs and notice which feel familiar.
  2. Take the self-quiz. Go with your first reaction.
  3. Try the daily practice for your result for one week.

There is no pressure to get it right. Notice what comes up, and take what resonates.

The five Clairs

"Clair" is French for "clear." Each one is a way your intuition can reach you. You likely have more than one, but one usually speaks first.

◉

Clairvoyance

Clear seeing

A flash of an image, a picture in your mind's eye, symbols, or colors that seem to mean something.

∿

Clairaudience

Clear hearing

A word or phrase that pops into your head, a song on repeat at just the right moment, a gentle inner voice.

♡

Clairsentience

Clear feeling

Gut feelings, chills, a heavy or light feeling in a room, picking up on other people's moods.

✦

Claircognizance

Clear knowing

You just know something, with no idea how or why, and it turns out to be true.

❀

Clairalience

Clear smelling

A scent with no source, like a loved one's perfume, cigar smoke, or flowers when none are around.
